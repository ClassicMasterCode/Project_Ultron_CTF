# Hints and scoring configuration

Recommended challenge value: 500 points.

| Hint | File | Cost |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 — Identity | hints/01_identity.txt | 50 |
| 2 — Authentication | hints/02_authentication.txt | 100 |
| 3 — Administrator access | hints/03_administrator.txt | 150 |

Maximum cumulative hint cost: 300 points.

CTFd's built-in paid hints deduct the specified cost from a user's score and will not unlock if the user does not have enough points. For a competition where this is the only challenge, provide a small warm-up challenge or implement a team-aware hint-credit/penalty mechanism. Test team accounting and alternate-account abuse before production.

Suggested display text: "Unlocking this hint deducts its listed cost from your competition score. Hint use is recorded for your team."
